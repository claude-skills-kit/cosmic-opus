# Audio credits

Everything in `audio/` was generated in October 2026 through [fal.ai](https://fal.ai):

- `vo/cosmic`, `vo/solar`, `vo/lessons`, `vo/extra`: Thai narration, **ElevenLabs Eleven v4** text-to-speech, voice "Jessica". Each take was cut into one clip per scene, tour stop or lesson tab. The scripts follow the on-screen captions (`cc19-app.js`), the tour cards (`solar-audio.js`) and the lesson texts (`solar-lessons.js`).
- `vo/solar-en`, `vo/cosmic-en`: English narration, **Kokoro** (model Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0) voice "af_heart", one request per paragraph.
- `sfx/`: sound effects and ambient beds, **ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2**, except `cc-moon.mp3` (radio static and Apollo-style Quindar tones, synthesised with ffmpeg).

Use of generated audio is subject to ElevenLabs' and fal.ai's terms. Keep this note with the files if you reuse them.
